## Q&A with the client (written answers)

1. **What is the main goal of the portfolio website?**
   A dedicated social media platform and professional networking hub for academic scholars. Its objectives:
   - **Host professional portfolios:** academics showcase diplomas, awards, certifications and career milestones.
   - **Share knowledge:** a free, accessible space to post published research papers, announce upcoming academic events and share new discoveries.
   - **Support student researchers:** research advisers publish and highlight their students' outputs and papers.
   - **Foster academic engagement:** a supportive community that helps scholars make their work more visible and connect over shared interests.

2. **How do you want your content to be presented?**
   (a) Creative details, for example personal quotes on how the research was made or conceived. (b) Ideas that set it apart from the norm.
   *Answer:* Besides the example in (a), more details will be added or polished in the next feedback sessions.

3. **Which features should be developed first (public-facing portfolio vs. private dashboard)?**
   *Answer:* The public-facing portfolio.

4. **How should diplomas, awards, certifications and research papers be categorized and prioritized?**
   Strictly prioritize academic and literary contributions, since the platform is an interactive research forum:
   - **Research & Literary Works (primary focus):** published papers, literary outputs and student research (posted by advisers). Most prominent on profiles and the main feed.
   - **Academic Credentials:** diplomas and degrees, in a dedicated "Academic Background" section that establishes the scholar's authority.
   - **Scholarly Achievements:** awards and certifications, filtered to academic excellence, research grants and relevant educational certifications, in a supplementary "Honors & Awards" section.

5. **Any extra features, such as letting other people post their papers?**
   *Answer:* Advertising academic events, research colloquiums and innovation projects.

6. **Who are the main users, and what should they be able to do?**
   *Answer:* **Research advisers** post their students' research papers. **Other users** engage with the posted content.

7. **Is developing in small parts (iterations) okay?** *Answer:* Yes.

8. **How often should progress be reviewed?** *Answer:* Bi-weekly.
