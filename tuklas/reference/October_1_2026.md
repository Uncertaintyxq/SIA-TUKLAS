## Decisions (aligned)

- **Split-layout login.** A purple brand panel on the left holds the logo, tagline and description. The login form sits on the right in a light card titled "Welcome back".
- **Role selection at entry.** Below the Login button, a "Continue as" row offers Student, Educator and Admin.
- **Purple and gold palette.** Six colors with set uses and role accents (table below).
- **Account creation link.** New users go to "Create your account" from the login card.

## Details

- **Login, brand panel:** Tuklas logo (yellow "T" badge), headline "From Defense to Discovery", a short description of the platform for students, educators and administrators, and a small gold badge.
- **Login, form:** a "Welcome back" card with a prompt to log in to the workspace, email and password fields (the password field shown focused with a highlighted border), and a purple Login button.
- **Login, role buttons:** "Continue as" Student, Educator, Admin. Admin uses the dark accent to stand apart.
- **Login, footer:** "New to Tuklas? Create your account".
- **Content priority on later screens:** research and literary works first on profiles and the feed, then "Academic Background" (diplomas, degrees), then "Honors & Awards" (awards, grants, certifications).
- **Platform rules to show in the UI:** student research appears only after a research adviser or academic panel approves it. Ads are limited to academic events: research colloquiums, school innovations, the C.P. Garcia event.
- **Development approach:** incremental, from basic to complex, public-facing portfolio first.
