# About This Manual

## Who It Is For

This manual explains how to use Tuklas, release 1.0, the research and academic engagement hub "From Defense to Discovery". It is for everyone who uses the site:

- **Visitors**, who read public research without an account
- **Students**, who build a portfolio, submit their works and take part in research groups
- **Educators** (research advisers), who review their students' works and run research groups
- **Admins**, the research office staff who look after their school on the site
- **Super-admins**, who look after every school on the site

You need no technical knowledge. Read Chapter 2 first, then the chapter for your role.

## How It Is Organized

- **Chapter 2, Getting Started:** creating an account, logging in, two-factor sign-in, finding your way around, and your account settings. For everyone.
- **Chapter 3, Visitors:** reading public portfolios, works and publications.
- **Chapter 4, Students:** your portfolio, your works from draft to approval, the feed and library, research groups, chat, events and opportunities.
- **Chapter 5, Educators:** reviewing works, your own publications, running research groups, and posting events, opportunities and announcements.
- **Chapter 6, Admins:** approvals, people, reports and moderation, and your school's departments.
- **Chapter 7, Super-admins:** the schools on the site.
- **Chapter 8, Troubleshooting and Questions**, and a glossary.

Educators can do everything students can, so Chapter 5 covers only what educators do in addition.

## Conventions

- Text that appears on the screen is written in double quotes, for example "Log in".
- **Select** means click with a mouse, or tap on a phone.
- In the pictures, numbered red boxes mark what to select or fill in. The numbers match the numbered steps next to the picture.
- Boxed notes, like the one below, give a rule or a tip.

> **Note:** Tuklas looks the same on a computer and a phone, except for where its menu is (section 2.10). The pictures in this manual are from a computer unless they say otherwise.
