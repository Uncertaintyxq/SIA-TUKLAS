# Troubleshooting and Questions

## Common Problems

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| The confirmation or password reset email doesn't arrive | Wait a few minutes and check your spam folder. Then ask for a new link; only the newest link works (sections 2.4 and 2.9). |
| "That link has expired" | Links work once, for 60 minutes. Ask for a new one. |
| Tuklas refuses my password | Use at least 12 characters. Tuklas also refuses passwords that have appeared in known data breaches; try a short phrase of unrelated words. |
| Tuklas refuses my two-factor code | Codes change every 30 seconds and work only once; wait for the next one. Check that your phone's clock is set automatically. |
| I lost the phone with my authenticator app | Log in with a recovery code (section 2.7). Students can then set up two-factor sign-in again; educators and admins keep using recovery codes (a known limitation of release 1.0). |
| I'm stuck on "Waiting for" my school | Your school's admins confirm accounts made without a school email, usually within a day or two. If it takes longer, contact your school's research office. |
| My work doesn't appear in the feed | A work becomes public only after your adviser or the academic panel approves it. Check its status on "My research". |
| I can't find a work someone told me about | It may be visible only to members of its school, or still waiting for approval. |
| A page says "This page isn't for your role" | That part of Tuklas is for another role, for example the review board for educators. |
| An educator can't review, publish or post announcements | An admin must verify the educator first, and can switch these rights on or off (section 6.3). |
| Notifications don't reach my phone | Select "Turn on notifications here" on that device (section 2.13). On iPhone and iPad, install Tuklas to the home screen first (section 2.12). |
| Chat or notifications don't update by themselves | Reload the page. If it keeps happening, tell the support contact. |

## Questions

**Who can see my work before it's approved?** Only its authors, its research adviser and the admins.

**Can I change who can read my work?** Yes. Open the work and change "Who can read it", even after it's approved.

**What happens to my work if I delete my account?** Approved works stay in the library under "Former member", so citations still work. Everything else that is yours is erased (section 2.13).

**Can I search inside chat messages?** No. Messages are stored encrypted. Find a chat by a person's, channel's or group's name instead.

**Why can't I create an admin account?** Admin accounts are issued by the research office, never created by sign-up.

**Where can I read the privacy notice?** At the address of your school's Tuklas site followed by /privacy (section 3.7).

## Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Adviser (research adviser) | The educator who reviews and approves a student's work. |
| Academic panel | The admins, approving works for the school's research panel. |
| Approval queue | The admins' page with everything waiting for a decision. |
| Authenticator app | A phone app, such as Google Authenticator, that shows the 6-digit codes for two-factor sign-in. |
| Channel | A topic inside a research group's chat. |
| Deliverable | Work handed in for a group deadline. |
| Draft | A work or publication that isn't submitted or published yet. |
| Educator | A faculty member who advises research. |
| Former member | How a deleted account is shown on the work it leaves behind. |
| Portfolio | A member's public academic page. |
| Publication | An educator's own research. |
| Recovery code | A one-time code for logging in without your phone. |
| Research group | An adviser-led group of students, with chat, files and deadlines. |
| Super-admin | An admin who acts for every school on the site. |
| Two-factor sign-in | Logging in with your password and a code from your phone. |
| Work | A student's research paper, literary work or innovation. |
