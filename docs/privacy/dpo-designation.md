# Designation of a compliance officer for privacy

> **Draft, 2026-11-04.** The operator of Tuklas, as a personal information processor, signs this to
> name the person responsible for its data protection (NPC Advisory 2017-01). Each school names
> its own Data Protection Officer, so it only needs to confirm that person in its data processing
> agreement (clause 4.2). Not legal advice.

**[Operator's name]**, operator of Tuklas ([https://domain]), names

**[Name]**, [position], [email], [phone]

as its **Compliance Officer for Privacy** from [date].

They shall:

1. Watch over how Tuklas processes personal data, and check that it follows the Data Privacy Act of
   2012, its rules, the NPC's issuances and each school's data processing agreement.
2. Keep a record of how Tuklas processes personal data, and a list of the sub-processors.
3. Lead the breach response team, and keep the yearly record of security incidents.
4. Help the schools' Data Protection Officers with requests from their people, privacy impact
   assessments and NPC registration.
5. Be the contact for the schools and the National Privacy Commission on Tuklas.
6. Advise the operator and the developers on privacy when Tuklas changes.

They act independently in this role, and have the access and information it needs. They shall not
hold another role that conflicts with it (for example, deciding alone on changes they then review).
